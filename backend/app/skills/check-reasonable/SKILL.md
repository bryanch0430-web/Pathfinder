# Reasonable visit

Apply these criteria on every review. A fail means the visit is not reasonable.

- A theme park, including Ocean Park, Disneyland, and Universal Studios, needs at least 5 hours (300 minutes). One, two, or three hours is not a visit.
- A hike needs at least 2 hours.
- A meal stays inside its meal slot.
- Two theme parks on the same day are not reasonable unless each still has its minimum and the clocks do not overlap.
- A free gap may be used to lengthen a stop that is already on the plan. That clock change does not need a new place.
- Do not add a stop and do not delete a stop. If the only way to make a visit reasonable is to add or remove one, name that stop and say the traveller must accept the change. Leave the plan as it is until they do.

`fix` is `lengthen` only when a longer clock on a stop already in the plan would make it reasonable. If the day has no free gap, `fix` is `none` and `issue` names the stop that would have to be removed, and that this needs permission.
