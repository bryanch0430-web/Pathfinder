Los Angeles is a collection of centres, not one walkable downtown. Plan by cluster: Hollywood and Los Feliz, Santa Monica and Venice, Downtown and Echo Park, or the museums around Exposition Park. Crossing the city between clusters in one day is the main planning mistake.

Traffic is a tax on ambition. Put two nearby stops together and accept that a third distant one may not fit. The Metro rail network is useful for a few corridors, especially Downtown–Hollywood and Downtown–Santa Monica, but many attractions still need a car, rideshare or bus.

The beach is weather-dependent. Santa Monica and Venice can be cool and grey in the morning; afternoon is usually better. Griffith Observatory and the Hollywood Hills are best around sunset, but the return trip can be slow.

Distances are large, so carry water and do not schedule back-to-back timed entries across town. Parking is expensive and often restricted; check signs carefully. Tipping is expected in sit-down restaurants, bars and taxis.
