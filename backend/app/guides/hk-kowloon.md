Kowloon is dense, layered and better in the evening. Tsim Sha Tsui's waterfront and the Star Ferry give you the classic harbour view; Temple Street and the surrounding Yau Ma Tei streets give you the market side of the city.

Do not try to cover all of Kowloon in one afternoon. Choose a corridor: Tsim Sha Tsui and Jordan for museums and the promenade, or Yau Ma Tei and Mong Kok for markets and street food. The MTR is fast, but the interesting parts are between stations, so plan short walks rather than a station-hopping checklist.

Temple Street is an evening stop. Arrive after the food stalls open, keep valuables close in the crowd, and pair it with a nearby dessert stop rather than a long taxi ride to another district. The promenade is best just before sunset, when the lights come on but the crowd has not yet peaked.

If you are staying on Hong Kong Island, do Kowloon as a half-day plus evening rather than crossing the harbour twice. The Star Ferry is part of the plan, not just transport.
