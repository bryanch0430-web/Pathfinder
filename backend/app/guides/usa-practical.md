The United States is large enough that “a USA trip” needs a region. A first visit usually works better as one coast plus one nearby region than as a checklist of New York, California and Florida in a week. Internal flights are common, but airport transfers and security time can consume half a day.

Tipping is part of the price in many service settings: sit-down restaurants, bars, taxis and rideshares. Sales tax is usually added at checkout rather than included in the shelf price. Carry a card, but keep some cash for small businesses and tips.

Entry rules depend on citizenship and passport. Check the current ESTA/visa requirement on an official government site rather than a search result. Travel insurance is worth considering because medical care is expensive.

Distances are in miles and temperatures in Fahrenheit. Accessibility varies by city and venue; check directly for step-free access and accessible restrooms. National parks often require timed entry or shuttle reservations in peak season. Book those before building the rest of the day around them.
