Central and Sheung Wan reward walking more than rushing between landmarks. The Mid-Levels escalator is a useful spine rather than a destination: ride it uphill, then walk down through the side streets around PMQ, Tai Ping Shan and Hollywood Road.

Victoria Peak is best treated as a weather-dependent stop. Go when the harbour is clear; if the peak is inside cloud, move it to another day. The Star Ferry is a better-value harbour crossing than a tunnel taxi, and the Tsim Sha Tsui promenade is the natural end of that walk.

Avoid scheduling a cross-harbour transfer between 17:00 and 19:00. Central's office crowds, the ferry queues and the MTR interchange all peak at once. A reasonable day is Central → Mid-Levels → Sheung Wan → ferry to Tsim Sha Tsui, with one museum or temple inserted rather than three.

Food in this area is better as a series of small stops than one long lunch. Save the sit-down dinner for Kowloon or Causeway Bay, and leave room for a bakery stop in Sheung Wan.
